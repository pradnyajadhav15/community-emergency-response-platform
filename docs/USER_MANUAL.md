# User Manual

CERP connects residents in a housing society with the people who can help them in an emergency: family guardians, neighbour volunteers, and security staff.

## Getting started

1. Install the CERP app (Android APK) or open it in Expo Go
2. Tap **Register**, fill in your details, and choose your role
3. Sign in. Your screens depend on your role
4. Allow **location** and **notifications** when asked

Ask your society administrator to assign you to your society and, for residents, your flat. Without that, alerts cannot be routed to your neighbours.

---

## Resident

### Raise an SOS

1. Open the **SOS** tab
2. Pick a category: Medical, Fire, Security, Accident, Fall, or Other
3. Optionally type what is happening
4. Tap the red **SOS** button, then **Send SOS** to confirm

Your flat and GPS location are attached automatically. The app tells you how many notifications were sent.

### Follow your alert

- The active alert appears as a card on the SOS tab. Tap it to open the incident
- You see who is responding, the escalation tier, and a chat thread
- Tap **Mark Resolved** when you are safe

### Set up emergency contacts

Open **Contacts** and add people at three tiers:

| Tier | Who | When they are contacted |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Primary guardian | Immediately |
| 2 | Secondary guardian | If nobody responds within the response window |
| 3 | Emergency contact | If there is still no response after the next window |

Add at least one tier 1 contact. If your guardian also uses CERP, ask your administrator to link their account so they receive app alerts as well as SMS.

### History

**My Alerts** lists every alert you have raised, with status and responder.

---

## Guardian

- Alerts from residents who list you appear under **Incidents** and in your **Inbox**
- Open an incident to see location, GPS, and the chat thread
- Tap **Accept & Respond** if you are handling it yourself, or use the chat to coordinate with neighbours

---

## Volunteer

- Keep **Available to respond** switched on in **Profile** when you can help; switch it off when you cannot, and you will not be routed new incidents
- New incidents in your society appear under **Incidents**
- Tap an incident, then **Accept & Respond**. Only one person can accept; if someone already has, you will see who
- Post updates in the chat, e.g. "On my way, 2 minutes"
- Tap **Mark Resolved** once the situation is handled

---

## Security staff

Same as volunteers, plus:

- You receive every alert in your society regardless of availability
- Use the incident chat to coordinate with volunteers and guardians
- Record what happened when resolving, so the incident has closure notes

---

## Society administrator (web portal)

Sign in to the admin portal with an administrator account.

| Page | What you can do |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Live totals, active alerts, average resolution time, alerts by category and status, notification delivery rate |
| **Alerts** | Filter all alerts; open any alert to acknowledge, escalate, resolve, or close it, read the notification log, and post in its thread |
| **Societies** | Create societies, add blocks to a society, add flats to a block |
| **Users** | Change a user's role or society, activate or deactivate accounts |

The dashboard and alert list refresh automatically every 15 seconds.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| App says it cannot reach the server | Check your internet connection. The server may be waking up; wait up to a minute and retry |
| Alert shows "location not set" | Ask your administrator to assign your flat |
| Neighbours are not notified | You are not assigned to a society; ask your administrator |
| No push notifications | Check notification permission in Android settings. Alerts still appear in the in-app inbox |
| "Already accepted by ..." | Another responder took the incident; you can still help via the chat |
